Oct 27, 2026 — 3:30pm • Percival Hall 209

Formal Power Series, Generating Functions, Connections, Tricks, Applications

Dr. Gerald Higdon (Professor at Fitchburg State University)
Combinatorics Special Functions
Read Abstract

This presentation begins with a careful definition of an abstract mathematical object called a ring. It then proceeds to show that the set of Formal Power Series forms a ring. To show this involves some tricky double infinite sums which are explained in careful detail. Multiplicative inverses in the ring of Formal Power Series are developed and two more “tricky” double infinite sums are considered. The talk concludes with the development of the exponential function and various properties of this function strictly from the theory of formal power series.
